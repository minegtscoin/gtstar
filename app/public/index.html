Why GTS

Full rules
  • 0% team, 0% presaleEvery GTS is mined by players.
  • Hard cap1,000,000 GTS at most. Rounds below 1 SUI mint less, so the final supply can end below it.
  • Buyback to stakers2% of every losing pot buys GTS on the market, and all of it goes to GTS stakers.
  • Liquidity that grows1% of every losing pot adds to the Cetus GTS/SUI pool: half buys GTS, then both halves go in together, locked in the game for good.
  • Wealth Fund grows with play4% of every losing pot (less the up to 0.005 SUI paid to whoever draws the round), and the whole pot less fees when no one is on the winning tile.
  • No halving shockThe reward falls a little every 15,658 rounds instead of halving.

How GTStar works

A 60-second game on a 5x5 board. Pick tiles, put SUI on them, and mine GTS every round.

  1. Pick tilesChoose one or more of the 25 tiles and put SUI on them. Minimum 0.0005 SUI per tile.
  2. Wait 60 secondsThe round runs for one minute. Deposits close 5 seconds before the end.
  3. One tile winsSui's on-chain randomness draws the winning tile. No one can see or change it in advance.
  4. ClaimWinners get SUI. Every SUI deployed mines GTS, win or lose.

What you win

  • On the winning tile: you get that SUI back plus a share of 90% of the SUI on the other 24 tiles, based on how much you put on the winning tile. You keep all of it, no cut.
  • On the other tiles: that SUI is lost, but it still mines GTS for you.
  • Up to 5 tiles per round. More tiles win more often, but the SUI on the other tiles is lost.

Wealth Fund

Part of every losing pot goes into the Wealth Fund, a jackpot that keeps growing, and more when no one is on the winning tile. Every round has a small chance to pay the whole fund to one player. Every round you lose earns you tickets, so the more you have played since the last payout, the better your chance.

What to do with GTS

  • Stake: earn SUI from every round and GTS from every buyback. Withdraw any time.
  • Trade: swap GTS for SUI at the best available price.
