Supply, emission and the reserve live in an immutable token contract. Nobody can mint extra GTS, create a team allocation or touch the reserve. The game contract can be upgraded, but it can never mint beyond the schedule or touch the reserve.

If no one deployed on the winning tile, the winners' 95% rolls into the Motherlode. Every round with a winner has a 1 in 100 chance to pay the whole Motherlode to the winning tile, split like the pot. The GTStar House wallet never keeps any of it.
