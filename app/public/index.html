Everyone mines

GTS is split across all participants by their share of each round. Winners and losers alike.

SUI-backed floor

4% of every losing pot flows into a reserve that backs every GTS. Burn GTS any time for your share of it.

Fair launch

1 GTS per round, halving every 6 months until 2030. No premine, no team tokens. Supply is locked in an immutable contract.

Provably fair

Winning tiles are drawn with Sui's native randomness. Every round is verifiable on-chain.

How it works

  1. DeployPlace SUI on one or more of the 25 tiles.
  2. Wait 60 secondsDeposits close 5 seconds before the round ends.
  3. One tile winsIts miners get their stake back plus 95% of the losing pot, pro rata.
  4. Everyone minesThe round's GTS is split across all participants.
