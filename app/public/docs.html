Fair by code.

GTStar is a mining game on Sui. You play, you mine GTS. No premine and no team tokens: every GTS is mined by players.

Intro

Why GTStar is the fairest game you can play.

Most tokens start with an insider advantage: a premine, a team allocation or a presale. GTStar has none. Like Bitcoin, every GTS has to be mined.

  • Fair launch. Zero GTS existed before the first round. Every token in circulation was earned by a player.
  • Capped supply, locked for good. At most 1,000,000 GTS will ever exist. Since 2026-09-30 the right to mint GTS is sealed in a separate contract that can never be changed, so no one can lift the cap, the owner included. The same kind of contract also limits minting to 2,000 GTS per UTC day, and the reward per round can only go down. Mining stops for good when the cap is reached, and burned GTS is never minted again.
  • Provably random. Winners are drawn with Sui's on-chain randomness. No server, operator or player can see or steer the result.
  • Every SUI mines. Every SUI you deploy in a round mines GTS, win or lose.
  • Buyback and burn. 3% of every losing pot buys GTS on the market and burns it for good.
  • Liquidity. 2% of every losing pot adds to the Cetus GTS/SUI pool: half buys GTS, then both halves go in together, locked in the game for good.
  • Non-custodial. The app holds no keys and no funds. Every action is signed in your own wallet. Auto Mine plays from a balance you set aside in an immutable vault, by a plan you signed, and only you can withdraw it.

Mining

Play a round, mine GTS.

DeploySUI on any of the 25 tiles
Wait60 seconds per round
DrawOne winning tile, drawn on-chain
ClaimSUI for winners, GTS for everyone

Rounds

  • A round starts with its first deposit and lasts 60 seconds. No deposits means no round and no emission.
  • Deploy SUI on up to 5 of the 25 tiles, minimum 0.0005 SUI per tile.
  • Deposits close 5 seconds before the end, so no one can react to other players at the last second.
  • When time is up, one winning tile is drawn on-chain. Anyone can trigger the draw.

Rewards

Winners get their stake on the winning tile back plus a share of 90% of everything deployed on the other tiles, split in proportion to what each player put on the winning tile. A win is a full win: you keep your whole share, no cut. Up to 5 tiles per round: each wallet plays a round with one miner, and all of its deposits in the round count together, so a wallet can hold at most 5 tiles. GTS is mined by the SUI you deploy, win or lose: the round's GTS is split by what each player deployed. Up to 1 SUI in a round, every 0.01 SUI mines 0.01 GTS (0.25 SUI mines 0.25 GTS).

losing_pot = total_deposits - winning_tile_total my_share = losing_pot * 90% * my_stake / winning_tile_total payout = my_stake + my_share round_reward = step_reward * min(round_total, 1 SUI) / 1 SUI my_gts = round_reward * my_deposit / round_total

Unrefined GTS

Mined GTS does not go straight to your wallet. It waits in your unrefined balance, and you withdraw it whenever you want. Each wallet has a 7-day clock that starts when it first mines and restarts at every withdrawal. After 7 days, withdrawing is free. Before that, the fee falls evenly from 10% to 0 over the 7 days. Mining more does not restart the clock. Half the fee is burned, so total supply falls, and half is shared by everyone who still holds unrefined GTS, by how much each holds; you get your part when you withdraw. If no one else holds unrefined GTS, all of the fee is burned. (Until 2026-09-30 all of the fee was burned.) Five GTStar wallets named in the contract (House, Bot 1, Bot 2, Bot 3, Matcher) get their GTS at claim, never pay the fee and get no part of it. Every other wallet, including GTStar's other bots, pays it like any player.

fee = 10% * time_left / 7 days (0 after 7 days) withdraw = my_unrefined - my_unrefined * fee + my_share_of_others_fees fee = half burned + half to everyone still holding unrefined GTS

Wealth Fund

The Wealth Fund is a jackpot in SUI that grows every round until one player takes all of it.

  1. It fills up. Every round, 3% of the losing pot goes in, less the draw reward (up to 0.005 SUI, see Fees). If no one is on the winning tile, the whole pot less fees goes in too.
  2. You collect tickets. When you lose SUI in a round, 10% of what you lost is paid in fees. Those fees are your tickets. The more you play, the more tickets you have. Nothing to buy or sign up for: tickets come on their own.
  3. It hits. Every round has a 1 in 250 chance to pay out. When it does, one ticket is drawn at random and its owner gets the whole fund, paid in SUI to their wallet.
  4. It starts again. After a payout all tickets reset to zero and the fund starts filling again.

You do not need to win your tile to take the Wealth Fund: a player who lost the round can take it. Tickets count from the last payout, so a player with 10% of all tickets has a 10% chance when it hits. Every ticket costs real SUI, so splitting SUI across several wallets gives no extra tickets. Six GTStar wallets named in the contract get no tickets (House, Bot 1, Bot 2, Bot 3, Matcher, Shield). GTStar's other bots are not on that list and collect tickets like any player. Tickets are added when your round is claimed.

my_tickets = 10% of the SUI I lost since the last payout my_chance = my_tickets / all_tickets (when the fund pays, 1 in 250 each round)

Auto Mine

Auto Mine plays rounds for you. You put SUI into your Auto Mine balance, pick a strategy and an amount per round, and every round is played from that balance until you stop it, the rounds you set are done, or the balance runs out.

  • Spread. 5 random tiles every round.
  • Sniper. 1 random tile every round, with the whole amount on it.
  • Hunter. The 5 tiles holding the least SUI, picked in the last 10 seconds before deposits close. A tile with less SUI on it gives each SUI on it a bigger share of the pot when it wins. That edge comes from the other players in the round, and it shrinks as more players use Hunter.

The tiles are picked on-chain: at random with Sui's randomness for Spread and Sniper, by the board at that moment for Hunter (ties are broken at random). No one chooses them, the keeper included. Spread and Sniper differ only in how often and how much they win, not in what they return on average.

Cost. At least 0.05 SUI per round. Of every round amount, 1% is paid to whoever runs the plan and 1% goes to the buyback and burn. The other 98% goes on tiles in your name and plays exactly like a deposit made by hand: it mines the same GTS, wins the same SUI and pays the same fees on a losing pot. GTStar's keeper runs the plans every round; the call is open to anyone, and whoever makes it gets the 1%.

round_amount = 0.05 SUI or more on_tiles = round_amount * 98% (0.049 of 0.05: mines GTS like any deposit) keeper = round_amount * 1% buyback = round_amount * 1% (burned with the 3% buyback)

Results. SUI you win goes back into your Auto Mine balance, so the plan keeps playing with it. Mined GTS goes to your unrefined balance, like any mined GTS. Each round is collected for you at the start of the next one.

Your balance. It sits in a separate contract, the Auto Mine vault, which is immutable: no one can change it, pause it or block a withdrawal, the owner included. Only you can withdraw, any amount, at any time, with the plan running or not. The only other way SUI leaves your balance is your own plan: exactly your per-round amount, at most once every 20 seconds, only while the plan is on and has rounds left, and never below the amount you chose to keep. You can also set a balance at which the plan stops by itself.

  • It is the same game. SUI on tiles that do not win is lost, and a plan can use up its whole balance. With few players in a round, most of what you lose goes into the Wealth Fund.
  • One miner per wallet and round. In a round you play by hand, Auto Mine does not play.
  • Wealth Fund tickets. Tickets from automatic rounds are added to the draw every 10 collected rounds, at once when you already hold the latest tickets, and when the plan has no round left to play. Until then they wait and do not count in a draw. A payout resets them, like all tickets.
  • Pause. If the game is paused, automatic deposits stop too. Withdrawing your balance never stops.
  • If the keeper is down. Plans wait and nothing leaves your balance. Anyone can run them, and the app lets you collect your own last round yourself.

Tokenomics

1,000,000 GTS, a gentle decay, a buyback that burns and growing liquidity.

Supply

Each round mints up to 1 GTS, shared by everyone in the round by SUI deployed, win or lose. The full reward needs 1 SUI deployed in the round; a smaller round mints a matching part (0.25 SUI gives 0.25 GTS). There is no halving: every 15,658 rounds the reward drops by 1.425%. The cut follows rounds played, not GTS mined, and what a round below 1 SUI does not mint is never minted later. If every round were full the schedule would mint about 1,099,000 GTS, so the 1,000,000 cap is only reached if rounds average above about 91% of 1 SUI. Otherwise the final supply ends below 1,000,000. There is no other source of GTS: no premine, no team tokens, no staking mint.

Locked limits. Two immutable contracts stand between the game and the mint: no one, the owner included, can mint past 1,000,000 GTS in total, or past 2,000 GTS in one UTC day (by the on-chain clock). A full day of 60-second rounds mints at most 1,440 GTS, so the daily limit never gets in the way of normal play. If it is ever reached, claims wait until 00:00 UTC; nothing is lost. The owner can still lower the emission (a smaller reward per round, a faster cut, shorter steps), which stretches the curve over more time, but can never raise it: the game refuses any change that would make a future round pay more than the current schedule.

reward = 1 GTS * 0.98575 ^ floor(rounds_played / 15,658) total <= 1,000,000 GTS (hard cap), stops earlier in rounds, lower in GTS, when rounds are below 1 SUI per UTC day <= 2,000 GTS (immutable daily limit)

Fees

Fees come only from the losing pot, never from a winner's stake.

Whoever draws a round is paid up to 0.005 SUI of it from the Wealth Fund share, then the liquidity share, so the draw pays for its own gas. The buyback, the stakers' 1% and the creator's 1% are never used for it. GTStar's own bots draw every round within seconds, so this is usually paid to them. In a round whose losing pot is under about 0.167 SUI, the whole Wealth Fund share goes to the drawer, and under about 0.1 SUI the whole liquidity share too.

Change on 2026-09-30: the split moved to more liquidity. Liquidity went from 1% to 3%, the buyback from 2% to 1%, and the Wealth Fund from 4% to 3%. Winners still get 90%, stakers 2%, creator 1%. Liquidity already spends about half its share buying GTS, so the buying stays about the same (2.5% of the losing pot), while three times as much SUI and GTS goes into the pool, so trades move the price less.

Second change on 2026-09-30: the buyback now burns. The buyback went from 1% to 3% and every GTS it buys is burned for good, instead of going to stakers. Liquidity went from 3% to 2%, and stakers from 2% to 1%, paid in SUI only. Winners still get 90%, the Wealth Fund 3%, the creator 1%, so all fees still add up to 10%. The withdraw fee now goes half to burning and half to everyone still holding unrefined GTS. GTS yield stakers earned before stays claimable. Transactions: game upgrade (transaction), stakers set to 1% (transaction).

Where lost SUI goes

This is a game of chance and the expected SUI return is negative. Of every losing pot, 7% leaves the game (3% buyback and burn, 2% liquidity, 1% to GTS stakers, 1% creator), and 3% goes to the Wealth Fund (less the draw reward), which pays it to one player when it hits. Using more tiles (up to 5) wins more often, but the SUI on the other tiles is lost every time, so a win can return less than you put in.

Staking

Stake GTS and earn SUI. Stakers share 1% of every round's losing pot in SUI, split by how much each staker has staked. Nothing is minted, so staking never dilutes holders. Withdraw any time. With nobody staked, the SUI share goes to the Wealth Fund. Until 2026-09-30 stakers also got the GTS the buyback bought; since then that GTS is burned, and any GTS yield earned before stays claimable.

my_sui_yield = round_share * my_stake / all_staked

Buyback and liquidity

Both shares are saved in the game every round and fixed in the current game code. The GTStar keeper spends them on the Cetus GTS/SUI pool, and only in ways the contract enforces.

  • Buyback and burn, 3%. The keeper buys GTS with the saved SUI, and the same transaction must hand the GTS back to the game, which burns all of it for good (buyback_burn_v2, event BuybackDone with the SUI spent and the GTS burned).
  • Liquidity, 2%. The keeper buys GTS with about half of the saved SUI, then adds that GTS and the other half to the pool as a new full-range position. The same transaction must hand the position back to the game, where it stays locked for good. No function can take it out, so this liquidity can never be pulled. SUI not used stays for the next add; GTS not used is burned with the next buyback.

The keeper buys back once 0.01 SUI is saved and adds liquidity once 0.1 SUI is saved, so gas stays a small part of each. Each buy moves the pool price by at most 2%; SUI it cannot spend under that limit stays saved for the next buy.

No reserve

GTS has no SUI reserve and cannot be redeemed for SUI. Its price is set only by the market, on the Cetus GTS/SUI pool. Until round 33, GTS could be redeemed from a SUI reserve. That ended on 2026-09-29, and the 12.29 SUI left in it moved to the Wealth Fund (transaction).

Verify

Don't trust, verify.

Three packages.Four packages. The supply lock holds the right to mint GTS and the 1,000,000 cap. The daily mint limit holds the only key to that lock and lets at most 2,000 GTS through per UTC day. The Auto Mine vault holds the balances players set aside for Auto Mine. Both are immutableAll three are immutable: their upgrade keys were destroyed, so nobody can ever change them. The game package holds the GTS coin type and the game (rounds, the draw, fees and emission). During the launch phase the owner can change game settings and fix game code at once, so bugs and exploits can be stopped right away.

Change on 2026-09-30: the GTS supply was locked. The mint authority (the GTS TreasuryCap) moved out of the game package into the supply lock, and the old Treasury object was deleted in the same transaction (transaction). The supply lock was made immutable before that (transaction). From now on GTS can only be minted through the lock, and it never lets the total ever minted pass 1,000,000. Everything minted before (72.93 GTS) counts toward the cap. The GTS coin type, balances, the pool and the game did not change.

Change on 2026-09-30 (later): the daily mint limit. The only key to the supply lock (its MinterCap) was sealed in a DailyLimiter from the immutable mint_limit package (transaction; the package was made immutable before that, transaction). No function returns or lends that key and the limit cannot change, so from now on at most 2,000 GTS can be minted in one UTC day, whoever holds the limiter and whatever code calls it. The same game upgrade made the emission one-way: set_emission can only lower the reward.

What the owner can do today

The owner address 0x51417aedc9cd847adc087d75a7d5a647fc1ea63744ac607c518b6c458c30bd4e holds two keys, with no timelock:

  • UpgradeCap (policy 0, the most open). It can publish new code for the game package at once. New code could do anything the game package can do, including moving SUI out of the game or the Wealth Fund, changing the fees and the rules, or deciding who receives GTS that is not mined yet (up to 2,000 GTS a day). The current code has no such function, but you are trusting the owner not to add one. What no upgrade can do: mint past 1,000,000 GTS in total, or past 2,000 GTS in one UTC day. Those limits live in the two immutable packages, outside the game package. An upgrade also cannot stop you withdrawing your Auto Mine balance, or take more from it than your own plan allows: the vault hands out only a player's own per-round amount, at most once every 20 seconds, and only while their plan is on. The game holds the vault's only pull key, so new game code could misuse those per-round amounts; stopping your plan or withdrawing ends that at once.
  • AdminCap. It changes settings within limits written in the contract. Each fee share it can set has its own cap (stakers 5%, Wealth Fund 10%), and the old reserve share is fixed at 0. The buyback and burn (3%) and liquidity (2%) shares are fixed. The buyback SUI stays in the game until the keeper spends it, and the same transaction must hand the GTS bought back to the game, which burns it (buyback_take / buyback_burn_v2); every buy emits BuybackDone with the SUI spent and the GTS burned. (Until 2026-09-30 the GTS went to stakers: buyback_keep, events BuybackKept and GtsYieldAdded.) The liquidity SUI works the same way: the keeper must hand back a Cetus position, checked by type, which the game keeps for good (liquidity_take / liquidity_lock, event LiquidityLocked). The owner cannot take either. Within the contract's limits the AdminCap can also set: Wealth Fund odds from 1 in 100 to 1 in 1,000,000, the tile limit from 1 to 25, the minimum deposit from 0.0005 to 10 SUI per tile, the round length from 30 seconds to 1 hour, the withdraw fee up to 50%, the emission (only down: a lower reward per round, a larger cut up to 50% per step, shorter steps; never up, never past the 1,000,000 cap or 2,000 GTS a day), and a pause of new deposits.

Current settings on-chain: Wealth Fund 3%, buyback and burn 3%, liquidity 2%, stakers 1%, creator 1%, the whole pot less fees to the Wealth Fund when no one is on the winning tile, odds 1 in 250, at most 5 tiles per wallet a round, withdraw fee 10% falling to 0 over 7 days (half burned, half to holders), emission 1 GTS per round falling 1.425% every 15,658 rounds. Every change emits a public event. The plan is to give up both keys once the game is stable, after which nobody can change anything. No date is set yet.

Locked for good, even against upgrades: the 1,000,000 cap (immutable supply lock), at most 2,000 GTS minted per UTC day (immutable daily mint limit), and every Auto Mine balance (immutable vault): only its owner can withdraw it, and nothing can pause or block that. Fixed in the current game code: the two Auto Mine fees (1% to whoever runs a plan, 1% to the buyback and burn), the 1% creator fee, the 3% buyback and burn, the 2% liquidity share, emission that can only go down, and no address can be blocked from playing, claiming or withdrawing. A pause only stops new deposits.

Known limits

  • The one-miner rule and the 5-tile limit work per wallet. Someone who covers the board with several wallets keeps the winning wallet's full share, so covering the board costs them only the 10% fees on the losing tiles. That only makes their GTS cheaper to mine: there is no reserve to take SUI from. Wealth Fund tickets come only from fees, so extra wallets give no extra tickets.
  • The daily limit counts by UTC day, not by rolling 24 hours: up to 2,000 GTS can be minted just before 00:00 UTC and another 2,000 just after. The emission can only go down, but the round length is a separate setting (30 seconds to 1 hour): shorter rounds mean more rounds, and more GTS, per day, always within the 2,000 GTS daily limit.
  • Five GTStar wallets are exempt from the withdraw fee in the contract, and six get no Wealth Fund tickets (see above). What the bots do with the GTS they mine is up to our off-chain bot code, not enforced on-chain.
  • Auto Mine needs someone to run the plans each round. GTStar's keeper does; if it stops, plans wait until anyone runs them. Running plans does not depend on GTStar, and neither does withdrawing: the vault needs no keeper.
