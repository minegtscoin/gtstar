Fair by code.

GTStar is a 60-second mining game on Sui. Players deploy SUI, one tile wins the pot, and every round mines GTS: a token with a hard cap, no premine, and a game that buys it back and burns it.

Overview

Why players and holders choose GTStar.

  • Fair launch. Zero GTS existed before the first round. No premine, no team allocation, no presale: every GTS in circulation was mined by a player.
  • Scarce by contract. At most 1,000,000 GTS will ever exist, and at most 2,000 can be minted in a day. Both limits are sealed in contracts that nobody can change, the owner included. The reward per round can only go down.
  • Deflation built in. Every round, 3% of the losing pot buys GTS on the market and burns it for good. The game does this itself, inside the draw.
  • Liquidity that cannot leave. 2% of every losing pot goes into the GTS/SUI pool and stays locked in the game for good. The trading fees it earns go back into the pool.
  • Provably random. Winners are drawn with Sui's on-chain randomness. No server, operator or player can see or steer the result.
  • Non-custodial. The app holds no keys and no funds. Every action is signed in your own wallet.

How to play

Play a round, mine GTS.

DeploySUI on up to 5 of the 25 tiles
Wait60 seconds per round
DrawOne winning tile, drawn on-chain
ClaimSUI for winners, GTS for everyone
  • A round starts with its first deposit and lasts 60 seconds. Minimum 0.0005 SUI per tile.
  • Deposits close 5 seconds before the end, so no one can react to other players at the last second.
  • When time is up, one winning tile is drawn on-chain. Anyone can trigger the draw.

What you win

Winners get their stake on the winning tile back, plus 90% of everything deployed on the other 24 tiles, split by what each player put on the winning tile. A win is a full win: you keep your whole share. More tiles win more often, but the SUI on your other tiles is lost every time.

losing_pot = total_deposits - winning_tile_total my_payout = my_stake + losing_pot * 90% * my_stake / winning_tile_total

Mining GTS

Every SUI you deploy mines GTS, win or lose. A round mints up to 1 GTS, shared by SUI deployed. It mints the full 1 GTS when it holds 7 SUI or more, and a matching part below that: 1 SUI mines about 0.143 GTS.

round_gts = 1 GTS * min(round_total, 7 SUI) / 7 SUI my_gts = round_gts * my_deposit / round_total

Unrefined GTS

Mined GTS waits in your unrefined balance and you withdraw it whenever you want. Each wallet has a 7-day clock: once it runs out, withdrawing is free. Before that the fee falls evenly from 10% to 0. Half the fee is burned, and half is paid to everyone still holding unrefined GTS, so patient holders are paid by early sellers.

The clock is weighted by amount. GTS you mine now starts its own 7 days, and your clock moves to the average of what you hold and what you just mined: 5 GTS with 3 days left plus 1 new GTS leaves 3 days and 16 hours for all 6. A withdrawal takes your whole balance. Every wallet pays the same fee, GTStar's own bots included.

fee = 10% * time_left / 7 days (0 once the clock has run out) withdraw = my_unrefined - fee + my_share_of_others_fees

Wealth Fund

The Wealth Fund is a jackpot in SUI that grows every round until one player takes all of it. It gets 3% of every losing pot, and the whole pot less fees when no one is on the winning tile. Every round has a 1 in 250 chance to pay the whole fund to one ticket, drawn at random.

Tickets come on their own: the 10% fee on the SUI you lose is your tickets, counted since the last payout. You do not need to win your tile to take the fund, and splitting SUI across wallets gives no extra tickets.

my_chance = my_tickets / all_tickets (when the fund pays, 1 in 250 each round)

Auto Mine

Auto Mine plays rounds for you from a balance you set aside. Pick a strategy and an amount per round (0.05 SUI or more):

  • Spread. 5 random tiles every round.
  • Sniper. 1 random tile, with the whole amount on it.
  • Hunter. The 5 tiles holding the least SUI, picked in the last 10 seconds.

Of every round amount, 1% is paid to whoever runs the plan and 1% goes to the buyback and burn; the other 98% plays exactly like a deposit made by hand. Your balance sits in an immutable vault: only you can withdraw it, at any time, and no one can change, pause or block that, the owner included.

The token

Hard to make. Bought back every round. Burned for good.

Supply

There is no source of GTS other than mining. The reward starts at 1 GTS a round and drops by 1.425% every 15,658 rounds, so there is no halving shock. At one full round a minute the supply takes about 5 years to mine, and rounds below 7 SUI mint less, so the final supply can end below the cap.

reward = 1 GTS * 0.98575 ^ floor(rounds_played / 15,658) total <= 1,000,000 GTS (immutable supply lock) per UTC day <= 2,000 GTS (immutable daily limit)

Fees come only from the losing pot, never from a winner's stake.

Built to hold value

GTS is designed as a store of value that a game keeps feeding. Five rules work for holders in every round:

  • Expensive to create. A round needs 7 SUI to mint 1 GTS. On average, mining one GTS costs about 0.67 SUI in fees, so new supply never comes cheap.
  • Bought back and burned. 3% of every losing pot buys GTS on the market and burns it in the same transaction. More play means more GTS gone for good.
  • It balances itself. The buyback spends SUI, so the lower the price, the more GTS it burns. Below about 0.20 SUI per GTS, a round burns more GTS than it mints.
  • Liquidity only grows. 2% of every losing pot is added to the pool and can never be pulled. Its trading fees are added back too.
  • Early sellers pay holders. Withdrawing mined GTS before 7 days costs up to 10%: half is burned, half goes to everyone still holding.

The ceiling is fixed as well: the 1,000,000 cap and the 2,000 GTS a day limit cannot be changed by anyone, and the emission can only be lowered. What these rules do not do is set the price. GTS has no reserve and cannot be redeemed for SUI: the market decides what it is worth.

Staking

Stake GTS and earn SUI: stakers share 1% of every losing pot, split by stake. Nothing is minted for it, so staking never dilutes holders. Withdraw any time.

How the buyback runs

No wallet holds the buyback or liquidity SUI, and no address can take it. The draw itself spends it on the Cetus GTS/SUI pool, before the winner is drawn:

  • Buyback. Once 0.005 SUI is saved, the draw buys GTS and burns all of it.
  • Liquidity. Once 0.05 SUI is saved, the draw buys GTS with 49% of it and adds the GTS with the matching SUI to a position locked in the game.
  • The 2% limit. One draw's buys can lift the price at most 2% above the price after the previous draw. Pushing the price up before a draw does not make the game buy higher: it buys less, and the rest waits for a later draw.
  • If Cetus stops. Rounds keep being drawn without the market step. After 7 days without a usable market, that SUI goes to the Wealth Fund instead, so nothing is ever stuck.

Locked for good

Three contracts are immutable: their upgrade keys were destroyed, so nobody can change them, the owner included.

  • Supply lock. No one can mint past 1,000,000 GTS.
  • Daily mint limit. No one can mint more than 2,000 GTS in one UTC day.
  • Auto Mine vault. Only you can withdraw your Auto Mine balance, and nothing can pause or block that.

What the owner can still do

The game contract is still upgradable during the launch phase, so bugs can be fixed at once. The owner address 0x51417aedc9cd847adc087d75a7d5a647fc1ea63744ac607c518b6c458c30bd4e holds two keys, with no timelock:

  • UpgradeCap. It can publish new game code. New code could change the rules and the fees, or move SUI held by the game or the Wealth Fund. It can never mint past the two locked limits, and it can never block an Auto Mine withdrawal.
  • AdminCap. It changes settings inside limits written in the contract: Wealth Fund odds, the tile limit, the minimum deposit, the round length, the withdraw fee (up to 50%), the stakers' and Wealth Fund shares (up to 5% and 10%), a pause of new deposits, and the emission, which it can only lower. It cannot change the creator fee (1%), the buyback (3%) or the liquidity share (2%).

Every change emits a public event. The plan is to give up both keys once the game is stable; no date is set yet.

Good to know

  • This is a game of chance. In SUI, the expected return of a round is negative: 10% of every losing pot goes to fees.
  • GTStar runs its own bots. They keep a round open around the clock and draw the rounds. Six GTStar wallets named in the contract get no Wealth Fund tickets; in everything else the bots play by the same rules as every player.
  • The 5-tile limit works per wallet. Someone who covers the board with several wallets pays only the fees on the losing tiles: about 0.67 SUI for each GTS mined.
  • The buyback and the liquidity add run on Cetus. If Cetus stops for good they end, and that share goes to the Wealth Fund.
  • Very small rounds pay the drawer less than a draw costs in gas, so GTStar's bots draw them at a loss.
