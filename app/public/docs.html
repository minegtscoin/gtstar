Intro

Why GTStar is the fairest game you can play.

Most tokens start with an insider advantage: a premine, a team allocation, a presale, or an admin key that can mint more. GTStar has none of these. Like Bitcoin, every GTS that will ever exist has to be mined, and the rules that govern supply are locked in code that no one can change, including us.

  • Fair launch. Zero GTS existed before the first round. There is no premine, no presale and no team allocation, and no function that could ever create one. Every token in circulation was earned by a player.
  • Fixed supply. The emission ceiling and the reserve live in an immutable token contract. The game's schedule mints at most 571,897 GTS, and the token contract itself refuses anything above its own ceiling of 572,003 GTS, whatever the game does. No upgrade, key or vote can raise that limit, and burned GTS is never minted again.
  • Provably random. Winners are drawn by Sui's native randomness beacon, produced jointly by the network's validators. No server, no operator and no player can see or steer the result in advance.
  • Everyone mines. Unlike single-winner games, every participant earns GTS every round, win or lose.
  • Backed by SUI. Any holder can burn GTS at any time for a share of the SUI reserve. Value that exists on-chain, not only on an exchange.
  • Non-custodial. The app holds no keys and no funds, and has no accounts. Your funds sit in public contracts and every action is signed in your own wallet. The only server feature is optional usernames: you sign a message in your wallet, the server checks that signature on Sui, and it stores nothing but the name for your address.

Mining

Play a round, mine GTS.

DeploySUI on any of the 25 tiles
Wait60 seconds per round
DrawOne winning tile, drawn on-chain
ClaimSUI for winners, GTS for everyone

Rounds

  • A round starts with its first deposit and lasts 60 seconds. No deposits means no round and no emission.
  • Deploy SUI on one or more of the 25 tiles, minimum 0.01 SUI per tile.
  • Deposits close 5 seconds before the end, so no one can react to other players at the last second.
  • When time is up, one winning tile is drawn. Anyone can trigger the draw: a keeper does it automatically within seconds, and the app shows a Draw winner button in case it is ever down.

Rewards

Winners get their stake back plus 95% of everything deployed on the other tiles, split in proportion to what each player put on the winning tile. Everyone in the round, winners and losers alike, mines GTS in proportion to their deposits.

losing_pot = total_deposits - winning_tile_total payout = my_stake + losing_pot * 95% * my_stake / winning_tile_total my_gts = round_reward * my_deposits / round_total

Supernova

If no one deployed on the winning tile, the winners' 95% is split in two: half rolls into the Supernova, a jackpot that keeps growing (named Motherlode in the contract), and half goes to the GTS reserve. Every round that has a winner also has a 1 in 25 chance, drawn on-chain with sui::random, to pay the whole Supernova to the winning tile, split by stake like the normal pot. The 4% reserve fee and 1% creator fee are taken as usual. The GTStar House wallet never keeps Supernova SUI: the contract sends its share straight back.

Fairness of the draw

The winning tile comes from sui::random. The draw runs in a private entry function, so no other contract can wrap it and cancel the transaction when it dislikes the result. The rules are the same for every player, and every result is recorded on-chain.

Staking

Hold GTS, earn more GTS.

  • Source of yield. Every round mints an extra 10% of its GTS reward for stakers.
  • Streamed. Each round's staker reward streams over 7 days, split by stake size, so yield accrues every second.
  • No lock-up. Stake, withdraw and claim at any time, or claim and restake in one step.
  • No sniping. Staking right before a round and leaving right after earns only for the seconds actually staked. Long-term holders get the rewards.
  • Nothing is lost. If no one is staked, the stream pauses and resumes when the next staker arrives.

Tokenomics

A fixed schedule, a halving, and a SUI floor.

Supply

Each round mints 1 GTS to miners and 0.1 GTS to stakers. Both halve every 262,000 rounds, like Bitcoin halves by blocks, not by date. After 7 periods (1,834,000 rounds) emission stops for good. At most 571,897 GTS can ever exist.

Rounds only happen when people play, so quiet periods stretch the schedule instead of losing GTS: a round every minute gives about 6 months per period, fewer players means longer. The immutable token contract also checks every mint against a time ceiling (1.1 GTS per minute, halving every 6 months), which the round schedule always stays under. The contract code also carries a 1,000,000 GTS safety limit, which the schedule never reaches.

Reserve

The reserve is SUI held by the token contract. Any holder can burn GTS for a proportional share, and SUI can leave the reserve in no other way.

redeem(x GTS) = reserve * x / total_supply // x GTS is burned floor_price = reserve / total_supply
  • Every round adds SUI and new GTS. The floor rises when a round adds more SUI per new GTS than the current floor, and falls when it adds less. Small rounds lower it, larger rounds raise it.
  • Redemptions never lower it. Reserve and supply shrink together, and rounding favours remaining holders.
  • Halvings help. Each halving cuts the new GTS per round, so the same SUI inflow lifts the floor more.

The floor is backed by assets actually held in the reserve. It is not a price guarantee and it moves with the balance between fees and emission.

Links

Don't trust, verify.

GTStar is split into two contracts. The token contract holds GTS, the emission schedule and the reserve, and is immutable: no one can ever change it. The game contract holds the board, fees and staking. It can receive fixes and improvements, but it can only mint through the token contract and within its schedule, and it can never touch the reserve. Every upgrade is public on-chain.
